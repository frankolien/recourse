# Product film craft: what the best fintech and software launch films do

Research done 2026-09-06 across Squads and Fuse, Mercury, Ramp, Brex, Stripe
Sessions, Linear, Arc, Cash App, Revolut, Monzo, Robinhood, Phantom, Coinbase,
Apple, and the 2025 to 2026 launches (MetaMask Money Account, Cash App Fall
Release, Robinhood Future Trading). Studios rarely publish easing numbers or scene
timings; the numbers below come from Apple's App Preview rules, paid-social ad
libraries and published motion standards, checked against what the films do.

## Structure and pacing

- 30 seconds holds 4 to 6 beats: 0 to 3s promise, 3 to 8s context, 8 to 18s
  method (2 to 3 connected steps), 18 to 26s payoff, 26 to 30s recall.
- The hook lands inside the first 1 to 3 seconds, before the scroll. Monzo and
  Revolut open on the product doing its thing or a question, never a logo.
- One workflow, not a catalogue. Cash App's Fall Release covers eleven features
  by giving each one beat: one screen, one line of text.
- Close on the strongest product frame plus the wordmark; the last 4 to 6 seconds
  must work as a still.

## Device framing

- Real UI, straight on, native resolution. Apple forbids implied functionality.
- Two modes: the UI animates inside a static phone (Linear, Mercury, Fuse, Brex),
  or the device moves in 3D with the UI still (Apple, Cash App, Robinhood). For a
  code-built film: the first, with a little of the second. Phone at 0 to 6 degrees
  on Y, 0 to 3 on X, drifting 2 to 4 percent over a scene.
- Scale: about 80 to 90 percent of frame height on a 16:9 frame with text beside
  it. Never crop the top of the phone.
- A real device silhouette with Dynamic Island reads as credible; an abstract
  rounded rectangle reads as a mockup. One soft contact shadow, no reflections.

## Typography

- Headline cap height 5 to 8 percent of frame height (60 to 90px at 1080p).
  Captions 3 to 5 percent. Two lines at most, 3 to 7 words per beat.
- Medium to Semibold headlines, Regular for the second line.
- Enter with a per-line rise (16 to 24px) plus opacity, or a baseline mask reveal.
  Per-word staggers at 30 to 80ms.
- Text lands first; the UI action follows 200 to 400ms later. Text exits before
  the next screen loads, never during.

## Motion

- Entrances ease out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1). Exits ease in:
  cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1). Repositioning: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1).
- In-app beats 200 to 320ms; film text entrances 400 to 600ms; exits at half.
  Scene holds 4 to 7s. Scale entrances from 0.95, never from 0.
- Transitions: cut, crossfade, or a ground colour change. No bounces above 0.2,
  no spins, no zooms into the screen. Animate transform and opacity only.

## Colour, sound, captions

- Consumer trust brands use light flat grounds; crypto-native brands use dark. One
  accent. The phone screen is the highest-contrast area of the frame.
- Design for muted playback. One instrumental track, at most three UI sounds.
- Burned captions inside the 90 percent safe area, never over the phone screen,
  on screen for 1s plus 0.3s per word. Ship a reduced-motion variant.

## Trust in fintech

- Plausible balances in the hundreds or low thousands, real transaction rows.
- One calm security beat: Face ID, three keys as three simple objects, one
  sentence on recovery. No fear cues.
- Say what the company is: a software company, not a bank or custodian.

## Rules we follow

1. Five beats in 30s: promise, context, method, payoff, recall.
2. The first frame is the product doing something; the claim lands by 1.5s; the wordmark comes last.
3. Real device silhouette, front on, 0 to 6 degrees of Y tilt, 80 to 90 percent of frame height, one soft shadow.
4. The UI moves inside a near-static phone; the phone drifts at most 2 to 4 percent per scene.
5. One headline per beat, 3 to 7 words, two lines at most.
6. Text rises 16px with opacity over 500ms on cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1); exits in 250ms on cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1).
7. Text lands first; the UI action starts 200 to 400ms later.
8. In-app beats 200 to 320ms; scenes hold 4 to 7s; no bounce, spin or zoom.
9. Transitions are cut, crossfade or ground colour change.
10. One ground and one accent; the phone screen is the highest-contrast area.
11. Real balance, real rows, real Face ID prompt.
12. One calm security beat.
13. Reads fully muted; one track; at most three UI sounds.
14. Captions never over the phone.
15. End on a real screen, wordmark and handle, held 3 seconds.
